What everything does

✨ Fix it for me

Listens to the track — how noisy it is, whether it's muffled or boomy, whether the volume jumps — and sets everything for you. Always start here.

Remove noise & hiss

An AI noise remover running on your own PC. Great on hiss and background noise. Too much makes music sound underwater.

Tone

One knob from warmer (more bass) to brighter (more treble). Muffled recordings want brighter; harsh ones want warmer.

Even out the volume

Two things: smoothing loud/quiet swings inside a track, and matching every track on the album to the same volume so nothing jumps.

Target sound (step 2)

Feed it good-sounding songs and ClearWave learns their tone, then steers your remasters toward it. Perfect for matching rough recordings to your studio ones.

A / B

Instantly hear the untouched original. Use it often — ears adjust fast and you may be doing more than you think.

Extra AI cleanup

Optional tools you install separately: stronger denoise, audience-noise removal, and vocal rescue. Green badges mean they're ready.

Advanced mode

Opens the full studio controls — EQ bands, compressor timings, limiter ceiling — when you want fine control.
